The Problem: The Margin Blur & Split Spreadsheets

A fast-growing Belgian distributor was managing operations across three siloed environments: an e-commerce platform, standalone warehouse spreadsheets, and desktop accounting software.

Because inventory receipts and supplier invoices were reconciled manually weeks after deliveries took place, the company relied on generic cost averages (AVCO). In a market with volatile freight rates and multi-supplier price shifts, this blurred actual profitability: management only discovered whether high-volume deals were truly profitable long after month-end closing.

The Operational Bottleneck: Without automated cost layers tied to physical picking lots, financial margins remain a delayed guess. Moving from manual reconciliation to real-time valuation ensures that every delivery slip instantly records its true cost of goods sold (COGS).

3 Key Operational Improvements

  • Automated Real-Time FIFO Costing: Odoo tracks inventory valuation lot by lot. When orders ship, the system depletes the oldest purchase layers first, posting the exact cost of goods sold (COGS) straight to the profit-and-loss ledger.
  • Automated Landed Costs (True Landed Margins): Overseas maritime freight and customs duties are distributed proportionally across incoming product receipts, ensuring inventory valuation reflects actual landed cost rather than basic factory invoices.
  • Direct Bank Reconciliation: Automated bank synchronization matches customer remittances against open invoices via defined reference rules, removing manual end-of-month reconciliations.

Key Takeaway: Accounting Tied to Physical Reality

Connecting the Dots: An ERP implementation succeeds when finance and logistics speak about the exact same physical movement at the exact same moment. Moving to automated FIFO valuation turned inventory management from a periodic guessing game into an exact operational balance sheet.
