The Systemic Bottleneck

A fast-growing distributor operated with three siloed software solutions: an e-commerce storefront, an unlinked warehouse management sheet, and desktop accounting software.

The critical flaw was asynchronous inventory state: the sales team regularly sold high-demand units that had physically departed the warehouse an hour earlier for another B2B client. These inventory race conditions led to emergency air-freight costs, backorders, and defensive email exchanges between sales reps and warehouse operators.

Core Insight: A physical warehouse is a state machine. If reservation events are not locked atomically at the quotation stage, your inventory database is merely a suggestion rather than a source of truth.

Integrated Flow Architecture

We unified the operations onto a single Odoo instance, replacing batch imports with automated reservation routes and automated ledger matching.

Key Technical Configurations

  • Automated Reservation Rules: Configured strict FIFO reservation rules upon order confirmation, distinguishing On Hand physical quantities from Forecasted available stock in real time.
  • Automated Landed Costs: Structured freight and customs cost absorption schemas to reflect accurate gross margins per product line.
  • Automated Bank Synchronization & Reconciliation: Replaced monthly manual matching with automated rule-based transaction reconciliation against Belgian bank statements.

Operational Outcomes

  • Atomic Stock Locking: Sales orders lock stock immediately upon confirmation, stopping sales reps from over-promising items that just left the warehouse.
  • Accurate Landed Costs: Inbound freight and customs charges are apportioned directly across received lots, revealing true gross margins per product line.
  • Daily Bank Reconciliation: Direct bank feed sync matched against open customer invoices replaced manual end-of-month accounting spreadsheets.
