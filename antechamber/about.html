Behind the Curtain

A quiet workshop outside client deadlines, design systems, and business metrics.

The main portfolio is built around operational outcomes: problem definitions, business constraints, shipped features, and measured impact. It is structured for clarity, efficiency, and professional evaluation.

The Antechamber is where the unresolved explorations live. It is a sandbox for audio experiments in Logic Pro, modular hardware repairs, speculative UI patterns, and unconstrained prototypes that are too raw or personal for an enterprise case study.

"Not every interface needs to optimize for friction-free speed. Some exist simply to question the default way we interact with sound, time, and software."
