The Sandbox

The other room.

The main portfolio displays what I ship and structure. The Antechamber is the workshop where things get disassembled, routed, and questioned.

This space is an uncurated sandbox outside client metrics and delivery sprints. Some days that means sound synthesis, mixer routing, and plugin chains in Logic Pro; other days it means prying open hardware with industrial adhesive, or building speculative interaction concepts just to test an intuitive hypothesis.
