About

Who I am

Systems thinker and Product Engineer. I operate at the intersection of business logic, interaction design, and frontend code — turning invisible friction into tactile software.

What drives me

I build to solve, not to perform. When a sales team bypasses an enterprise tool six times a day, or when a user closes a task app out of frustration, that is not user failure — it is a signal that the underlying model was designed against human intuition.

Three years across enterprise ecosystems (SAP S/4HANA and Odoo) taught me that system architecture is never just technical; it is about cognitive clarity. Today, I bring that same instinct to building my own software: taking complex workflows apart, stripping away unnecessary abstractions, and engineering tools that feel responsive, deliberate, and calm.

My approach

  • Friction-first diagnosis: Watch where people hesitate, where workarounds emerge, and design for the actual human behaviour rather than the idealised process.
  • Rapid execution & agentic engineering: Leveraging AI coding tools to move from conceptual architecture to deployed, interactive prototypes in days instead of months.
  • End-to-end craft: Balancing systemic constraints (state synchronization, business rules, edge cases) with tactile interaction details (keyboard shortcuts, timing, visual hierarchy).
