The Engineering Mindset: Opening the Box

Whether prying open a sealed smartphone enclosure with a heat gun to replace a battery, mapping complex signal chains in Logic Pro, or diagnosing why an enterprise inventory system desynchronizes under peak load: I refuse the black box.

I believe the best digital tools are built by people who understand both ends of the spectrum:

  • Under the hood (Systems Thinking): Relational databases, state machines, atomic reservation locks, and predictable data pipelines.
  • In the hands (Human Interface Craft): Zero-latency interactions, keyboard-first ergonomics, tactile gesture controls, and clean mental models that respect human attention.
Core Conviction: Without an opinionated frame, every to-do list and enterprise process collapses under cognitive debt. Software should impose calm, deterministic structure rather than passive chaos.
