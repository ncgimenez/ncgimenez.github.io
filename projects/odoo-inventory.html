The Problem: Disconnected Tools and Ghost Stock

A fast-growing distributor was running their business on three separate tools: an online shop, an unlinked spreadsheet in the warehouse, and desktop accounting software.

Because these tools weren't talking in real time, the sales team regularly sold items that had physically left the warehouse an hour earlier for another client. This created expensive rush shipping, backorders, and constant blame games between sales reps and warehouse operators.

The Core Rule: Stock must be locked the second an order is confirmed. Without real-time reservation, the inventory number on a screen is just a guess—leading two sales reps to accidentally promise the exact same box to different clients.

3 Key Improvements We Put in Place

Why FIFO (First In, First Out) instead of Average Cost (AVCO)?
• In the warehouse: Odoo automatically reserves the oldest inventory lots first so goods don't sit forgotten on high shelves.
• In accounting: If you buy Batch A at $10 in January and Batch B at $14 in February, FIFO calculates your true margin lot-by-lot, rather than blurring everything into an average. The finance team always knows the real profit margin on every shipment.
  • Instant Stock Reservation (No Double-Selling): The moment a sales rep confirms a quote, Odoo locks the physical pallet in the warehouse. Sales reps can clearly see what is physically on the shelf (On Hand) versus what is already promised (Forecasted), ending emergency backorders.
  • Automated Landed Costs (True Margins on Day 1): When imported goods arrive, overseas shipping fees and customs taxes are automatically added to the product cost. Finance knows the true margin immediately, instead of discovering surprise carrier bills weeks later.
  • Automated Bank Reconciliation: Daily bank feeds match incoming payments directly against open customer invoices using simple matching rules, replacing manual month-end Excel reconciliations.

Key Takeaway: Operations as a Single Pipeline

Connecting the Dots: An ERP rollout succeeds when sales, warehousing, and accounting talk about the exact same physical box at the exact same second. When the software handles inventory locks automatically, sales reps and warehouse operators never have to argue about who promised what.
