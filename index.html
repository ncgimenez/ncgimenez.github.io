SNCB Transit Connections — Spatial UX & Commuter Wayfinding

The Challenge

Catching a tight 4-minute rail connection induces severe commuter stress. Standing at the wrong carriage door upon arrival at Brussels-Midi turns an easy transfer into a missed connection.

The System

Cross-referenced rolling stock carriage vestibule layouts directly with station platform staircase and escalator coordinates, determining optimal transfer positioning 5 minutes prior to arrival.

The Interface

High-contrast 2D schematic cutaway aligning carriage doors directly with platform descents, reducing passenger walking distance to zero and eliminating orientation friction before the train stops.

SAP S/4HANA Order-to-Cash — Enterprise Systems Architecture & Governance

The Challenge

During a high-stakes ERP transformation, sales teams bypassed core software using rogue offline spreadsheets for multi-currency pricing, resulting in compounding revenue leaks and month-end ledger chaos.

The System

Audited data bottlenecks, aligned cross-functional requirements, and engineered atomic pricing condition records directly into S/4HANA with automated validation rules and billing guardrails.

The Interface

Frictionless standard order entry flows that align user speed with strict accounting governance, eliminating shadow spreadsheet workarounds and driving audit discrepancies down to 0.0%.
